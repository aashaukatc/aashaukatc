## About

I turn complex healthcare revenue operations into systems that teams can measure, govern, and improve.

Across 12+ years supporting U.S. healthcare, my work has covered billing, A/R, denials, eligibility, authorization, payer operations, credentialing, client implementation, analytics, automation, and workflow optimization. That frontline depth now informs my work in solutions architecture, business analysis, product-oriented delivery, and revenue intelligence.

My strongest contribution is connecting operations, data, and execution: diagnosing where work or revenue is getting stuck; translating the problem into requirements and controls; building decision-ready reporting; and supporting implementation through UAT, adoption, escalation, and measurable follow-through.

Current areas of focus include healthcare RCM leadership, Epic and multi-platform revenue-cycle workflows, operational analytics, data governance, implementation, product operations, and AI-assisted workflow design.
