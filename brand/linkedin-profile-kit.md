## LinkedIn About Section

I build practical systems that help organizations see where revenue, workflow, data, and execution are breaking down — then convert that visibility into measurable action.

My foundation is U.S. healthcare Revenue Cycle Management and operations. I have worked across billing operations, denial management, payer follow-up, eligibility, authorization, AR, provider/facility performance analysis, and client-facing reporting. That operating background now drives my work in data systems, decision-support architecture, workflow governance, and AI-assisted execution.

My work is focused on four outcomes:

1. Making hidden revenue leakage visible.
2. Turning messy operational workflows into clear systems.
3. Building data models and dashboards that support real decisions.
4. Creating proof-of-work products that show execution, not theory.

Current public work includes:

- DIRT — Data Intelligence for Revenue Transformation, a healthcare RCM intelligence direction focused on denial patterns, revenue leakage, payer behavior, workflow risk, and no-PHI governance.
- AynaMate — a privacy-first beauty-services marketplace direction focused on service discovery, booking workflows, partner operations, and staged platform architecture.
- Sehatyaab — a healthy nourishment product system combining market research, segmentation, nutrition-card UX, direct-order commerce, and brand-operating logic.
- Masla Yeh Hai — a Pakistan-first civic education and public intelligence platform for structured issue-based learning.
- RCM Data Platform — reusable analytics foundations for payer, CPT, provider, denial, AR, TAT, and performance analysis.

I am best aligned with roles, collaborations, and client work involving healthcare RCM leadership, operations intelligence, business analysis, workflow redesign, dashboard systems, product operations, AI-assisted automation, and research-to-product execution.
